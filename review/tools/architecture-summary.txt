=== Entry Points ===
Flask app: app.py (main entry point, serves /api/*, templates)
CLI scripts in scripts/:
scripts/analyze_di.py
scripts/train_a2.py
scripts/validate_a2.py

Cloud worker: cloud/kaggle/train_a2_cloud.py

=== Main Modules ===
hybrid/core/ - audio DSP (render, envelope, blend, alignment, calibration)
hybrid/modes/ - design modes (hybrid, fixed_blend, character_blend, training targets)
hybrid/continuous_gain/ - CG tab (multi_blend, project, validation)
hybrid/training/ - A2 training (local, kaggle, validation, export)
hybrid/services/ - auxiliary (settings, LLM, update check, NAM inspector)
routes/ - Flask routes
static/js - Browser UI (src/modes, src/routes, src/ui)
